## New

- **Autopilot Style setting** (Mod Settings, with the other Autopilot settings). Drone, the default, flies as before: the car leans forward to speed up, pulls back to slow down and banks into bends. Spinner keeps the nose level: the car still banks into bends, and the thrusters alone speed it up and slow it down. Both fly the same routes at the same speeds. With a Mod Settings too old for lists it shows as a switch: on is Spinner.

## Upgrading from 0.4.10

- Install over 0.4.10; your settings are kept, and the Autopilot stays in Drone style until you change it.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
