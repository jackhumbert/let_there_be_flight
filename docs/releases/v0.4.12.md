## Fixes

- **Arasaka Tower 3D black screen.** Starting the hidden arcade game in the Badlands church turned the screen black, with your normal HUD still showing and the controls locked. The game plays as its own character whose HUD is the game's screen, and LTBF was giving that character your HUD instead; it no longer does. The same fix keeps Johnny's sequences and the tutorial on their own HUDs. The flight HUD is now added to your own HUD, so a mod that changes the HUD keeps the flight HUD too.
- **No crash at take-off on a car with many added parts.** A car that another mod had already filled with parts (Immersive Car Modification, for one) could crash the game a few seconds after take-off or when the Autopilot engaged. Such a car now flies without its thruster meshes, with its wheels showing.

## Upgrading from 0.4.11

- Install over 0.4.11; your settings are kept.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
