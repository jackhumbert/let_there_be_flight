## What's new

- **Autopilot.** While flying, hold the autodrive key (G, or L3 on a gamepad): the vehicle takes off, flies a planned route between the buildings to your tracked destination and lands. With no destination it roams the city; tap the key to switch between the two. Any flight input takes over for a moment, and the camera key gives the cinematic camera.
- **Landing pads.** The city's AV pads are on the map and the minimap, discovered by flying near them, with supplies the first time you touch down on one. Track a pad and the Autopilot lands on it.
- **Flying summons.** Summon your vehicle on or near a landing pad and it flies in and lands on the pad. Where it cannot drive to you it flies the rest and lands next to you. "Always Fly Summoned Vehicles" (off by default) makes every summon fly.
- **Standard, the new mode flight starts in.** Every control asks for a speed; let go and the vehicle comes to rest, level, where it is. Handling (Loose, Default, Tight) and Brake Strength set the feel.
- **Hover & Fly** is still there, second in the mode list, flying as it did up to 0.3.20.
- **New sound.** Synthesised thrusters and wind, played through the game's own audio, with echoes off the ground and walls and reverb that follows the space you fly through. `fmodstudio.dll` is gone, and with it the 126 / 127 errors.
- **Ground effects.** Sparks, dust, trails and a scrape sound for each surface when the vehicle touches the ground. Drone mode rests and slides on the road on a soft skid.
- **A city you can fly through.** About 1,200 hidden colliders removed, and solid walls on towers that were drawn without any.
- Spanish text for all the settings.

## Fixes

- Top speed is bounded by air drag, and flying vehicles no longer drift upwards.
- The third-person camera no longer pumps in and out while flying; the first-person head sway is damped in every mode.
- Thruster sound comes back after dying and loading a save, mutes in menus, and is no longer silenced by a tunnel.
- A teleport or fast travel in flight holds the vehicle until the place has loaded.
- A hook the game refuses no longer hangs the game at startup.
- Input Loader 0.2.4 is required, the first release that reads game 2.5 inputs.

## Upgrading from 0.3.20

- Delete the old `red4ext/plugins/let_there_be_flight` folder first, or let your mod manager do it.
- The settings menu was reorganised. Your Hover & Fly on/off choice is kept. Brake Strength, Handling, Gamepad Flight Toggle Hold, the Brake key (C: both brakes are one key now), Drone Turn Speed and Drone Thrust start at their defaults.
- Hover, Fly and Anti-Gravity Drone are behind one setting, Extra Flight Modes (off). Automatic mode is gone. Previous Mode is J.
- Gamepad: with a hold on the flight toggle, move the toggle off L3, which vanilla autodrive and the Autopilot also hold.

## Known issues

- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
