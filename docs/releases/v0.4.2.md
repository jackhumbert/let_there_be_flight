## What's new

- **A better cinematic camera in flight.** The Autopilot's cinematic camera was made for cars on a road, and in the air many of its shots did not work. Now:
  - The shots that ride along with the car stay level and turn with its heading. They no longer swing round the car every time it banks into a turn.
  - The car sits a little off centre with room ahead of it, and the camera stays far enough back to keep it in frame however hard it banks, without drifting in and out.
  - Flybys are set up beside the route the Autopilot is about to fly, so the car sweeps past at a steady pan instead of flying through the camera, and they last long enough to watch. A flyby that would be blocked by buildings is skipped.
  - The camera mixes its shots more, avoids cutting to nearly the same view, and skips shots that would put the camera inside a wall. The wheel and hood close-ups, which ended up inside the car's outline in the air, are left out.

## Fixes

- The Autopilot no longer takes off again for a quest objective after reaching a map pin. Once it has arrived it finishes the landing (or the hover) and hands back, as vanilla autodrive does.

## Upgrading from 0.4.1

- Install over 0.4.1; your settings are kept.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
