## What's new

- **Your flight route for navigation mods.** While you fly with something tracked, LTBF now shares the route to it with other mods: the route the Autopilot flies when it is engaged, or one it plans for you when you fly by hand, planned again when the destination moves and when you stray from it. A mod that draws the GPS path can draw this one instead of the road route. [In-World Navigation](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/4583) uses it: with both installed, its arrows and the minimap's GPS line follow your flight route while you fly. LTBF draws nothing itself, so without such a mod nothing changes. For modders: the script API is `FlightGuidance` (see `docs/design/guidance.md` on GitHub).

## Upgrading from 0.4.5

- Install over 0.4.5; your settings are kept.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
