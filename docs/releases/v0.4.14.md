## Fixes

- **Fixed the crash on cars with hundreds of added parts.** A car that another mod had filled with parts (Immersive Car Modification, for one) could crash the game a few seconds after take-off, when the Autopilot engaged, or while loading. The game can only keep track of 256 parts attached to one point of a car and crashed past that, with or without LTBF; LTBF now keeps track of the rest itself.
- **Cars with many added parts have their thrusters again.** 0.4.12 left the thruster meshes off such cars to stay under that limit; with the limit gone, they fly with their thrusters like any other car.

## Upgrading from 0.4.13

- Install over 0.4.13; your settings are kept.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
