## What's new

- **Choose when landing pads are marked in the world.** A new setting, Landing Pad Markers in the World, under Landing Pads: Always, In a Vehicle (the default) or Flying. The pads' markers no longer show on foot unless you pick Always. The map and minimap pins are unchanged.

## Fixes

- Summoning again while your summoned car or bike is already out no longer brings a second one. If it is on its way it carries on; if it has landed near you, nothing happens; if it is far away, it takes off and flies back to you.
- The brake now stops a fast car in a reasonable distance. Above about 50 m/s (180 km/h) it bites harder, so a car at top speed stops in a few hundred metres instead of about two kilometres. Braking at ordinary speeds is unchanged.
- LTBF no longer crashes the game at start-up when another mod adds a broken vehicle record (a malformed TweakXL yaml). The broken record is skipped.
- LTBF's HUD now carries game 2.5's changes to the base HUD. It had been made from the 2.31 HUD, so a few 2.5 scenes could miss HUD parts they expect.
- The Autopilot no longer stops in mid-air short of its destination on a long trip. When the rest of the route was late, the car could stop at the end of its first leg and stay there; it now waits for the route and flies on.
- No more thump in the wind sound at each cut of the Autopilot's cinematic camera.
- The Enable Flight hint no longer shows while vanilla autodrive or its cinematic camera is driving the car, since flight cannot be switched on then.

## Upgrading from 0.4.2

- Install over 0.4.2; your settings are kept.
- If you liked the landing pad markers on foot, set Landing Pad Markers in the World to Always.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
