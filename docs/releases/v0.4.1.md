## What's new

- **Numbered landing pads.** Every landing pad has a number of its own, shown in its map pin and messages ("AV Landing Pad 14") and painted on its deck in place of the old "28" sign.

## Fixes

- The missile lock diamond and other target markers show again with LTBF installed. LTBF had registered its own copies of some game types, which broke the game's checks on the tracked target.
- The whitelist and blacklist examples in the readme and in `vehicleFlight.tweak` now work: the yaml needs one line per vehicle, `Vehicle.<record>.canEnterFlight: true` (or `flightDisabled: true`). The nested form the examples used to show is rejected by TweakXL ("Unknown property" in its log), so nothing was listed.

## Upgrading from 0.4.0

- Install over 0.4.0; your settings are kept.
- If you made a whitelist or blacklist yaml from the old example, rewrite it one line per vehicle as above.

## Known issues

- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
