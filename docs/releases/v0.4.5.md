## What's new

- **A ready-made whitelist of the high-end cars.** For anyone who wants flight on only the expensive cars: [`whitelist_high_end.yaml`](https://github.com/jackhumbert/let_there_be_flight/blob/main/examples/whitelist_high_end.yaml) on GitHub lists the hypercars and sports cars V can own (Rayfield, Herrera, Quadra, Mizutani Shion, Porsche 911, Yaiba Semimaru, Militech Hellhound), and their traffic copies. Copy it into `r6/tweaks` and set Flight Availability to Whitelist Only. Borderline cars (the Villefort and Chevillon sedans, the Hozuki, the bikes) are in it as lines you can switch on by removing the `#`.

## Fixes

- A car that another mod or a quest spawns is no longer flown to V. LTBF flies a summoned car in when it cannot reach V, and it treated any summoned car that way, not only the one you called. With a mod that respawns your car in a parking spot by the apartment, the car took off and flew up into the garage ceiling. Now only the car you summoned yourself, for a minute after you call it, is flown in.

## Upgrading from 0.4.4

- Install over 0.4.4; your settings are kept.

## Known issues

- A few flybys still swing round quickly when the car strays from its planned line or speed.
- Ride-along shots close to buildings can end after a second or two when the camera touches a wall.
- The Autopilot can wander off its line for the first seconds of a route; engage it from a hover where you can.
- A landing on a pad can end turned away from the pad's heading.
- Drone sliding along a road can still be thrown where two road pieces meet at an angle.
- With a destination the Autopilot cannot reach, the vehicle hovers and no message says why.
