# Tools

One folder per job. Python scripts put `tools/` on `sys.path` and import shared code from
`common/`; machine paths come from `common/paths.py` (each overridable by an environment variable).
Offline tools never touch the game.

| Folder | What | Inputs, outputs |
|---|---|---|
| `common/` | `navmap.py` (the one navmap.bin reader), `paths.py`, `toolpath.py` (sibling folders on sys.path) | |
| `navplan/` | `navplan.cpp` (the planner offline, CMake target `navplan`; `NAVPLAN_TRIP` flies the whole trip, `flightsim/Trip`), `follow.cpp` (the follower alone on a point mass, for `ltbf_tests follow_` and `autopilot_fleet_follow`), `bench.py` (benchmark routes, compare builds or parameters; also flies each as a whole trip: landed, time, off the line, speed and landing error, touchdown, replans, rays, the trip checks), `tune.py` (Optuna search), `measure.py` (shared by both), `fleet.py` (the benchmark routes planned and flown as whole trips with every distinct car of `tools/flightsim/vehicles.tsv`: the Autopilot's spread over the fleet and the cars at its ends; `tests/autopilot_fleet_tests.cpp` is the offline check, `ltbf_tests autopilot_fleet`), `ahead_sweep.py` (plan-ahead horizon and margin), `dips.py`, `plot.py`, `city.py`, `navviz.py` (planner's view over a recorded run), `routes.json`, `vehicles.json` | navmap.bin, routes; CSV, PNG, video |
| `navmap_bake/` | the nav map bake: `sectors`, `entities` (street furniture placed as entities: lamps, gantries, railings), `geometry_cache`, `presets`, `proxies`, `glb`, `rasterize`, `preview`, `spans`, `navmap`, `doorways`, `gates`, `boundaries` (the game's world boundaries and the quest facts that gate them), `publish` (uploads a bake to cp2077_navmap and pins it in `CMakeLists.txt`); steps in its README | game archives; navmap.bin, `src/navigation/gates.tsv`, `src/navigation/boundaries.tsv` |
| `world/` | city collision: `blockers.py` (hidden colliders), `proxy_colliders.py` (+ `physx_cook.py`; its `walls` step finds proxy walls under a real roof with no collision, for the force list), `collision_at.py` (what collision is in a box, down to the actor), `collider_cases.json` (known hidden and real colliders), `opened_patches.py` (space a bake change opened: hollow building or open air), `proxy_collider_force.json` (buildings given collision by hand) | sectors; `src/navigation/blockers.txt`, collider entities |
| `pads/` | landing pads: `pads.py`, `pad_*.py`, `flat_spots.py`, overrides and sector placement (`pad_sector.py`) | sectors, navmap.bin; `src/navigation/helipads.tsv`, pad entities, our sector |
| `acoustics/` | `flyby.py` (a recorded flight heard with echoes and reverb from the nav map), `probe.py` (rays through the nav map), `render.py` (the echo pairs and reverb), `acoustics.cpp` (the plugin's acoustics code offline, CMake target `acoustics`), `compare_features.py` (its features against flyby.py's), `compare_render.py` (its render against flyby.py's, per octave), `compare_live.py` (the in-game log of live collider against nav map distances), `stems_video.py` (a run's video with the plugin's FMOD stems as its audio tracks, `--extra` for a synth render), `engine_synth.cpp` (the engine synth over a run's thruster parameters, CMake target `engine_synth`), `engine_fit.py` (the synth against the FMOD event on the same run, per control held), `engine_variants.py` (a run's video with the synth's switches each way as tracks); `docs/design/engine-synth.md`; `docs/design/acoustics.md` | navmap.bin, Autopilot telemetry, `tools/acoustics/assets`; WAV, PNG, MP4 |
| `flightsim/` | Hover & Fly and Drone flown offline on the plugin's mode, input and force code (CMake target `flightsim`; `tests/feel_tests.cpp` checks the fleet in `ltbf_tests feel`): `Sim` (the body, ground and FindGround model; `--mode hoverfly|assist|drone|agdrone|droneassist`), `Scenarios` (the beginner situations, Drone's `drone_*` ones, and their metrics), `Replay` (a game recording's pieces flown offline beside the game's), `Trip` (a whole Autopilot trip: `Navigation::Autopilot` from the engage to landed on `Sim`'s body through the force tail, over a nav map with modelled plan delay, boxes for parked cars and props streamed in, floor contact, knocks and teleports; `CheckTrip`, the rules every trip keeps; `ltbf_tests autopilot_trip` and navplan's `NAVPLAN_TRIP` fly it), `flightsim.cpp` (runs them, `--vehicles` for every car of a survey table, `--replay`), `Colliders` (the city's collision as triangles for rays: `Sim::groundRay`'s FindGround and underside contact; `docs/design/hover-fly-terrain.md`), `PhysXWorld` (the game's own PhysX 3.4 in flightsim: its DLLs loaded from the game's bin/x64 at run time, NVIDIA's PhysX-3.4 headers to build against, `LTBF_PHYSX34` in CMake; built only when they are there; CDPR's PxShape differs from the public header, see the file) with `flightsim --terrain ... --physx` (each line's ground within 30 m as one PhysX mesh per mesh id of the region, the car its thruster pods and chassis box) and `flightsim --physx-joint` (a body sliding over flush mesh joints), `Scenarios`' `drone_skid_*` (Drone's skid: set down tilted, dropped, up a folding road; `--set skid`, `skidPerThruster`, `skidTurn`, `pointContact` (the contact at points, so a corner down turns the car), `sphereContacts`, `sphereRadius`, `openBottom`), `Seams` (where a region's drivable ground meets without a shared edge, the step or gap a car meets there: `flightsim --seams <region>`; flush joints between meshes, which threw Drone, share their corners and are not among them), `region_pack.py` (a box of the bake's collision, with a mesh id per triangle from the bake's shape ids (region file version 2), less what the plugin moves away, plus the terrain and LTBF's proxy colliders, as a region file for `Colliders`; `--regions` packs `terrain_regions.tsv`; run with `python -P -E`), `TerrainCheck` (a game recording of the terrain runs against a region: `flightsim --ground-check`, `--replay --region`; `terrain_compare.py` runs both over a folder of recordings and sums them up), `TerrainRuns` (lines over real ground: each line's ground profile and tags, a sampler, a driver holding speed and heading; `flightsim --terrain terrain_runs.tsv` flies them, `--vehicles` over the fleet, `--sample <region> <n>` draws lines), `aero.py` (drag from each car's chassis collision shape: front, side and top area, top speeds under drag models; the chassis box and underside profile for the contact; prototype, `docs/design/autopilot.md` "Character"; run with `python -P -E`, it needs numpy), `tweak_physics.py` (the car table from TweakDB, grouped by physical car, plus the spawned measurements TweakDB lacks; `--geometry` fills the cars nobody surveyed), `rig_geometry.py` (thruster tensor, wheel offsets, bottom and thruster count from each entity template and its rig, nothing spawned; the configuration and its thruster bones from `thruster_slots.py`'s parser), `thruster_slots.py` (ctest `thruster_slots`: every vanilla car and bike's configuration finds the bones and wheel slots its thrusters need, sane positions, no wheel left showing; `--report` for the audit), `vehicle_slots.py` (regenerates its fixture `vehicle_slots.tsv` from the game files after a patch), `fleet.py` (every distinct surveyed car, the spread of each metric and the cars at its ends; `--mode` as flightsim's; `--terrain` flies the terrain runs and sums them up by car class, kind of ground and speed), `vehicles.tsv` (the survey); `docs/design/hover-fly-feel.md` | scenarios, `vehicles.tsv`, `ltbf_hoverfly_*.csv`; metrics, CSV |
| `telemetry/` | `autopilot.py` (the Autopilot's per-frame CSV), `aerobatics.py` (`[AutopilotA]` log lines), `surge.py` (how much a flight held the surge, run against run), `fit_surge.py` (the car's surge share and drag from full-surge frames) | telemetry; summaries, plots |
| `physx/` | `pxscene_vtable.py` (PxScene's vtable slots in the game's PhysX3_x64.dll: raycast, sweep, overlap) | the game's PhysX DLL |
| `packaging/` | `pack_archive.py` (run by the build), `icons/` (map pin and AUTOPILOT logo), `physx_cook/` (the C++ PhysX cooker) | archive source; the .archive |
| `maintenance/` | `minidump_addrs.py` | |
